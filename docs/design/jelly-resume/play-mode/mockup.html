Play mode · UI mockup, round 1

Jelly Resume, 2026-10-14. The binding contract for the Play HUD, the touch controls and the summit card. The page in each screen is the real snapshot at play zoom; Phill is a placeholder silhouette (in the build he's the 3D model, with the llamas). Pink numbers match the notes under each screen.

What we agreed

  • Career Climb. Start on the bottom line (Guitarist, 1998) and climb to your name. The resume is newest-first, so climbing it bottom-up walks the career in order. Dated job lines are checkpoints; the name is the summit (a dance and a spark firework, your time against your best).
  • Impact coins. The 27 numbers the Finder's Impact chip finds (#metrics) glow green. Smash one: +1. A recruiter who plays reads the numbers.
  • Smash = instant burst into comet sparks, sprayed the way he punches: the attack clip's strike, ~60 ms hit-stop, a small shake, and burst()'s shockwave knocks the neighbors loose.
  • Every line is a jump-through platform. Jump up through lines, land on tops, ↓ drops through, ↓ in the air is a ground-pound. Smashed words leave holes that heal. Page edges drop into the void: he flails (die clip) and respawns at the last checkpoint. No lives, no game over.
  • Scale and camera. He's ~0.5 units tall in Play (about 3 lines; 3.4 in orbit). Follow-cam: ~90 px tall on desktop, ~60 px on a phone, leading the way he faces, moving vertically when he lands on a new line. Yaw 0, a slight tilt.
  • Sound. Small synthesized jump, smash, land, coin and summit sounds through sfx.ts, only while Sound is on (clicks outside Play stay silent).
  • Music. Words keep dancing (the platforms bob to the beat). Standing still ~1.5 s with music on, he dances; any input stops it. walk, attack and jumping are excluded from the dance rotation by name.
  • Clips. Run = the game's walk sped up. Jump = Mixamo jumping, scrubbed by the jump's state (wind-up skipped), its hip rise stripped (physics does the lifting). Smash = the strike window of the 2.87 s attack.
  • Off while playing. Page gestures (rotate, pan, pinch, knock), the Finder, the navigator, the HUD bar, the black hole, Chaos/Burst/Heal. Shroom mode keeps working (it drops words: the floor crumbles). Shrimp stay in the backdrop.

Later, not v1: shrimp as stompable enemies · gamepad · a Mixamo “Running” clip (only if the sped-up walk looks like speed-walking) · leaderboards (need a backend).

  4. Key legend (bottom-center): visible on entering, fades ~4 s after the first move; ? shows it again. Also A/D run, ↑/W jump, S drop, K smash. The resume's other shortcuts are off while playing except M (sound) and P (music).
  5. Coins: the Impact numbers glow green and pulse gently (a shader tint, like the Finder's highlight). Smash one: “+1 IMPACT” pops, the count bumps, a polite live region says “Impact 8 of 27”. It re-forms as a plain word (collected: no glow).
  6. Smash (X): the strike of the attack clip. Words in front of him from hips to chest (about two lines), about one long word ahead, burst into comet sparks sprayed the way he punches; ~60 ms hit-stop, a small shake. The shockwave loosens the neighbors (chain reactions for free). Here: ~71K-resource (a coin), AI, an, a.

  8. The site's ♫ stays on desktop (nothing's under it but the page). P still plays/pauses.
  9. The level: every line is a jump-through platform; ragged line ends are ledges; the gap before a right-aligned date is a pit; holes heal (2.2 s burst + 1.1 s re-form). Off the page edge: the void (flail, respawn at the last checkpoint). Only words at home (or springing back) hold him; loose words are debris he shoves.

Touch buttons

AA▶▶

Idle · pressed. Glass at 50% so the climb shows through; never under 44 px.

Checkpoint toast

✓ CHECKPOINT2021Front End Developer · Designit for Microsoft

One per dated job line, in climb order: 1998, 2018, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2026. The summit replaces the last.

Falling off

Off a page edge → the die-clip flail into the nebula (~1 s) → he drops back in at the last checkpoint with a splat. The timer keeps running.

No lives, no game over: falling just costs time.

Reduced motion

The engine never loads, so there's no Play mode: the plain /resume page as today.

Play adds no new reduced-motion path.
