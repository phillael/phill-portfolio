# Round 2 audition: sound credits

Every candidate in `audio/` is **CC0 1.0** (public domain; no attribution required, credited here anyway):

- **Juhani Junkala, "The Essential Retro Video Game Sound Effects Collection [512 sounds]"** (files `*-junkala-512.mp3`): https://opengameart.org/content/512-sound-effects-8-bit-style. Its `INFO.txt`: "released under CC0 creative commons license. You can do anything you want with these sounds."
- **Kenney, "Music Jingles"** (`*-kenney-music-jingles.mp3`) and **"Digital Audio"** (`*-kenney-digital-audio.mp3`): https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles, https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio. Their `License.txt`: "License (Creative Commons Zero, CC0) … Credit (Kenney or www.kenney.nl) would be nice but is not mandatory."
- **celestialghost8, "Victory"** (`finish-2-celestialghost8-victory.mp3`): https://opengameart.org/content/victory (CC0 on the page; no license file in the download).

`shrimp-announce.mp3` is Phill's own (a Logic Pro bounce, from Number Slayers).

Not usable in this public repo: Number Slayers' other sound effects (SoundMorph, IndieSFX and Epic Stock Media libraries, whose licenses forbid redistributing the files), including `eat-correct.mp3` and `shrimp-shower.mp3` ("Magic spell - sparkle shield", Epic Stock Media).

Each file was trimmed of silence, made mono, peak-normalized (about −4 dB; the smash −5, the landing −8) and encoded as a 64 kbps mp3.
