§4The shroom trip: what "things get weird" means

Eating the mushroom turns on the resume's own shroom mode for 12 s (not the site's global one: the wizard is hidden under the resume anyway, and it would stay on after you leave Play). On top of today's look (the paper goes night violet, the words go neon and hue-cycle, they melt and droop), Play gets weirder (try Eat the mushroom in §2):

  1. NEWPsychedelic molten: on top of the forge colors below, rainbow bands sweep across the page through the metal: the white-hot cores stay, the mid-tones take the neon hue, the edges cool to violet, and the glow on the paper is neon too. Some words run forge-orange while others run rainbow, changing as the bands pass.
  2. NEWSwelling words: every half second or so a word near you swells (to ~1.7–2.2×) or, now and then, shrinks (to ~0.65×), shoving its neighbors aside with a jelly wobble over 2–3 s, then settles; up to three at once. In the engine it's real: the swelling word's lattice gets new spring targets (its off) that grow it, and its neighbors' targets push away, so the jelly does the shoving and you ride the swollen words (your footing follows the lattice).
  3. NEWThe lasagna turns giant: it grows 4× and can't be hurt for as long as the trip lasts (§3, Giant): the mushroom is a power-up and a dare. Your moon jumps are how you get away.
  4. Molten words: the ink heats like metal in a forge: deep red at the edges, orange, white-hot in the thick strokes, with an orange glow on the night paper and a heat shimmer. Each column of letters slumps its own amount and drips; the drips narrow to a point and cool to red. It heats up over the first 1.5 s and cools back to neon, then ink, as it wears off. The lines you stand on don't move (the drips are paint, not physics). In the engine: the words' shader already drips in shroom mode (uMelt/uDrip); this adds the heat colors, the glow and longer drips while the trip is on.
  5. Moon jumps: jumps go 1.5× higher and float down slowly (gravity × 0.6). New lines are in reach.
  6. Rainbow Phill: his neon rim cycles through the hues and he leaves a trail of three fading afterimages.
  7. The camera sways: a slow roll of ±3° and a breathing zoom (±4%) on a 6 s cycle. Gentle on purpose.
  8. The page breathes: the sheet's waves swell (3×) and the words dance as if the music were loud, music or not.
  9. The floor stays: the random word drops of shroom mode are off while playing (a power-up shouldn't delete the level under you); his smashes still work.
  10. Wearing off: in the last 2 s he flickers (2.5 Hz, under the 3 Hz flash limit) and the power-down sound plays; the look eases back over 1 s. Another mushroom resets the 12 s.
  11. HUD: a 🍄 pill with a draining bar beside the stats; the live region says "Shroom mode for 12 seconds" / "Shroom mode over".
  12. Already in shroom mode (site or HUD toggle)? The mushroom still adds the Play weirdness for 12 s; the look stays on after.

Optional extra, your call: Big Phill (Mario's real mushroom): he grows to 1.6× for the 12 s and bursts any word he walks into.
