Mockup notes

Try

  • Drag the hall (or swipe on a phone), use the arrows, or pick a name under it. The set in front powers on and plays; the others idle.
  • Click the lit screen (or Watch): the picture lifts out of the glass as a hologram, crops to the full video, and the old set becomes its projector. Esc, ✕ or a click outside puts it back.
  • The mock bar: what the idle sets show, how much CRT, sound effects, and reduced motion.

The sets

Each relic is on the set the museum of 4789 got wrong: Pong on a 1974 wood console, Jelly Resume in a space helmet set, Rezmancer on a beige office terminal, Number Slayers on a black tube set, Pickleball 2079 on a red portable, Quadzilla on a pocket TV (its screen is tall, for the phone video). Built in code here; production could keep them or swap in real models.

Your videos

  • 30 s, 16:9 (1080p master is plenty), action from the first frame. Keep the key action inside the middle 4:3: the CRT crops the sides, the hologram shows all of it.
  • Quadzilla: a phone screen recording, portrait.
  • Sound is optional. With sound, the hologram gets a Sound button (off by default) and the site music pauses while it plays.
  • I encode them (H.264 + AV1, about 2–3 MB each) with poster frames.

Production

  • Every project stays real text (tabs + panels) for search and screen readers; the 3D only presents it.
  • The scene loads near the section, renders only on screen, and decodes one video at a time.
  • Reduced motion: no camera moves, power-on or static; video plays only on request. The Pause button stops all motion (WCAG 2.2.2).
