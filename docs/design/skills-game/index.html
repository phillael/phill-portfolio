Skills rethink · Round 1

Your skills, as a game

The old section is gone. Both ideas share one new section: AI first in a glass panel, the rest as quiet lines, in the Experience section's look. Press Play and it becomes a game made of your skills. Same list in both (skills.js), built from your resume and both handoffs.

  • A

    Skill Breaker

    A brick breaker where the skills are the bricks. Press Play and the AI words fly out of the list into level 1. Levels are your skill groups, AI first. AI bricks take two hits and drop their own power-ups: Claude Code splits the ball into three subagents, MCP servers widen the paddle, prompt caching doubles points, extended thinking slows time, and human-in-the-loop lays a net that saves one miss. Mouse, touch drag or arrow keys.

    Play →
  • B

    Type Invaders

    Space Invaders where you shoot by typing. Your skills march down in waves, AI first; type a skill's name to lock on and blast it, letter by letter. The one game where playing means reading every skill. On a phone, tap a skill to zap it.

    Play →
