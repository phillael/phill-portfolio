| `riser-perc.mp3` | [Percusive Noise Riser](https://freesound.org/s/261415/) | Stereo Surgeon |
| `riser-uplifter.mp3` | [FX Uplifter 100](https://freesound.org/s/204758/) | maqsim |

The crashes and booms are cut to 3.5 s at most (fade-out 0.6–1 s). Each riser is cut to end where it arrives (its last moment within 6 dB of its loudest; the uplifter keeps its last 6 s), so the engine can land that end on the drop and start a longer one part-way in.

Made from Freesound's high-quality previews (about 180 kbps mp3; the original WAVs need a Freesound login): cut (the rides to 3 s at most with a 0.6 s fade-out), peak-normalized to −1 dB, and re-encoded as VBR mp3 (`-q:a 1`). For the site, download the originals from the same pages and run the same steps on them.
