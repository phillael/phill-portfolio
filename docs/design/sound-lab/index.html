Sound lab

Every sound is synthesized live: oscillators, filters, envelopes and noise, except the recorded drums and 8-bit cues you pick (CC0). Change anything and it’s saved in this browser (the Skill blaster mockup picks it up, live if it’s open in another tab). When it sounds right, Copy patch and send it over. Once baked (into round6/synth.js’s defaults, then bake-skills-config.mjs), the Skill blaster bank is the site’s sound.
